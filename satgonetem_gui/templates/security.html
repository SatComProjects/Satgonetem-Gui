Space Security Lab

Training range. Attacks are confined to the emulated constellation. Do not point these tools at real networks. The GUI is a local teaching tool with no authentication.
Project: not loaded Containers: stopped Addressing: not applied Routing: not applied

0. Reconnaissance

Read-only. Map the topology, find bottlenecks, and choose a target before sending any attack traffic.

Mission

  1. Find the lowest-capacity links in the table. Those are the bottleneck candidates.
  2. Click a link row to inspect its endpoints, capacity, delay, and state. Add it to your evidence.
  3. Pick an addressable target and inspect its interfaces, loopback, and routing table in the dossier.
  4. Write the target card: why this target, what the attacker gains, and which path or link you expect to use.
  5. Export the dossier for your lab report.
